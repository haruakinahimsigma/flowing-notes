ABOUT THE AI

Flowing Notes uses Spotify's open-source Basic Pitch model to detect polyphonic notes. It runs on your device, so your uploaded audio isn't sent to our server.

Best results: clear piano recordings with limited background instruments. Mixed songs may produce extra or missed notes.
