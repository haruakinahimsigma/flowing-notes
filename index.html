ABOUT THE AI

Flowing Notes uses a high-resolution piano transcription model for note onsets, note offsets, velocity and sustain-pedal events. Audio is sent securely to the configured Flowing Notes inference service for processing.

Best results: clear piano recordings with limited background instruments. Mixed songs may produce extra or missed notes.
